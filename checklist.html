What is on the list

  1. Replace the furnace filter and check the fit (EPA's MERV 13 guidance)
  2. Test every carbon monoxide alarm
  3. Start a radon test; about 1 in 10 Wisconsin homes is elevated
  4. Check indoor humidity against EPA's 30 to 50% range
  5. Clean the dryer vent
  6. Run the furnace and smell the first cycle
  7. Look inside a supply register and a return grille
  8. Confirm bath and kitchen fans vent outdoors
  9. Clear the mechanical room
  10. Schedule the professional checks
