What air duct cleaning costs in Madison, and what drives the price

By Nick Bellino, NADCA ASCS · Ducts On Call, Madison WI · Updated 2026-10-23

The honest answer is that a proper whole-system cleaning of an average Madison home is a several-hundred-dollar job, and anything advertised for $99 is not a whole-system cleaning.

What a real cleaning involves

A complete cleaning means the furnace and every supply and return run are placed under negative pressure, agitated, and vacuumed out; every register and grill is removed and washed; and the blower compartment and plenums are cleaned. That takes a properly equipped crew several hours. Per-vent pricing, add-ons for the furnace or AC coil, and a dryer vent are the usual components of a quote.

What moves the price

  • Number of vents and returns. A 1,200 sq ft ranch and a 3,000 sq ft two-story with two zones are different jobs.
  • Furnace and coil. Cleaning the blower and the evaporator coil adds time but is where a lot of the dust actually sits.
  • Condition. Post-renovation drywall dust, pet hair, or a previously uncleaned 40-year-old system take longer.
  • Access. Finished basements and tight mechanical rooms slow the work.
  • Add-ons. Odor elimination, bio-growth treatment, and dryer vent cleaning.

The $99 special

Low advertised prices are a lead-generation tactic. The crew arrives, finds "mold" or "problems" and the price multiplies, or they vacuum at the registers for 30 minutes and leave the trunk lines untouched. The EPA warns homeowners about companies that make sweeping claims about health benefits or recommend duct cleaning as a routine part of maintenance, and NADCA explicitly advises against blow-and-go pricing.
